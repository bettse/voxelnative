Map settings (map_meta.txt)

Paste the server's map_meta.txt to use its noise settings. The default is VoxeLibre's mapgen v7 as shipped.



This seed is 263 or more, and the stronghold rings then depend on how the server's CPU converts it to an integer. Pick the one the server runs on; the other choice is a different set of rings. Seeds below 263 convert the same way everywhere.

Terrain and biomes follow Luanti's generator (mapgen v7 and the biome picker) bit for bit, and landmarks follow the engine's decoration placer and VoxeLibre's placement rules; both checked against a headless render of the same seed. A village marked with ? is a chunk where the game attempts one; whether it is built depends on terrain checks the map approximates. Caves that open at the surface are not carved.

North (+z) is up and east (+x) is right, as on the game's minimap. Overworld sea level is y = 1. Hover or tap for height and biome; drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, double-tap to zoom in.

The Nether view shows what you would see from above its roof: the lava sea where it is open to the air, otherwise the lowest floor above the lava (brighter the higher it sits), and dark where the rock is solid. Biomes and the cave shapes follow the engine's noise; nether outposts, bulwarks, bridges and ruined portals follow the game's placement rules on those floors. A portal links overworld x,z to nether x,z divided by 8; switching realms moves the view accordingly.
