Map settings (map_meta.txt)

Paste the server's map_meta.txt to use its noise settings. The default is VoxeLibre's mapgen v7 as shipped.


Stronghold positions come from the seed through a 64-bit integer cast that the two CPU families do differently. Pick the one the server runs on; the other choice is a wrong set of rings, not a slightly different one.

Terrain and biomes follow Luanti's generator (mapgen v7 and the biome picker) bit for bit, and landmarks follow the engine's decoration placer and VoxeLibre's placement rules; both checked against a headless render of the same seed. A village marked with ? is a chunk where the game attempts one; whether it is built depends on terrain checks the map approximates. Caves that open at the surface are not carved.

Overworld sea level is y = 1. Hover for height and biome; drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.
