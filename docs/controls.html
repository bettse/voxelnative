Controls

You look around with your head, like any visionOS app. Everything else comes from a pair of PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers or a Bluetooth keyboard (with your gaze doing the aiming). The keyboard keys follow desktop Luanti's defaults where they can, so desktop habits carry over.

PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers

Turn both controllers on before launching if you can. A controller paired later still works; it just shows up a moment after it connects.

You aim with your head: the block highlight and what you dig or place follow the centre of your view. With a bow or crossbow in hand, a ring shows where the arrow will land.

Inventory, chests and other panels

Panels (inventory, chests, furnaces, the crafting table, the recipe book) float in front of you. Point at them with the right controller (or your gaze, if it isn't tracked).

InputDoes

Text fields (signs, the recipe book search) open an on-screen keyboard you type on with the trigger. "done" submits, like pressing Enter.

Look-and-pinch also works for clicking: pinch while looking at something, like pressing Enter. A paired trackpad or mouse doesn't reach an immersive game on visionOS today, so plan on the keyboard or the controllers.

Pause menu

Right Options (or Esc) opens it. Move with either stick or the arrow keys, pick with a trigger or Enter.

  • Resume closes the menu.
  • Take photo saves a level 4K shot from where you're looking to your Photos.
  • Coordinates turns the game's position readout on or off (VoxeLibre's /whereami). It shows x, y and z in the top-left corner, handy in the Nether where a compass just spins. The server remembers the setting.
  • Exit to menu leaves the server and goes back to the launcher.
  • Quit game closes the app.
