Privacy policy

Last updated September 27, 2026.

VoxelNative doesn't collect anything. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no server run by me. The app talks only to the Luanti game server you type in.

What goes to the game server you pick

  • Your player name and password. The login uses Luanti's standard SRP handshake, so the password itself is never sent, only a proof that you know it.
  • What you do in the game: your position, where you look, what you dig, place and craft, and your inventory moves. That's how any Luanti client plays.
  • That server is run by whoever runs it, not by me. What it logs or keeps is up to its operator and their own policy.

What stays on your Vision Pro

  • Your saved servers (address, port, player name and password) and your settings (volume, view distance).
  • Downloaded game media (textures, sounds, models) cached so the next join is faster.
  • A diagnostic log in the app's Documents folder. It never leaves the device unless you copy it off yourself.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Sensors and permissions

  • Head and world tracking render the world from where you're looking.
  • Hand and controller tracking put your hands and controllers in the world.
  • Local network lets you join a server on your home network.
  • Photos, add-only: photo mode and video clips save to your photo library. The app can't see or read what's already there.
  • None of this sensor data is saved or sent anywhere. The only thing the server gets is the in-game position and look direction the game needs.

Chat

The app doesn't send or show chat. Other players on a server can't message you through it, and you can't message them.

Kids

The app doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone, kids included, because it doesn't collect anything.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page changes, with a new date at the top. Questions go to the GitHub issues.
